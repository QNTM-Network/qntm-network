The electron travels the ring, and settles at the tail.

One and a half revolutions, clockwise, in 700ms on the sheet's declared default curve, arriving at (71,71) — the tail position that makes this a Q and not an O. The mark is app/mark/qmark.html and app/mark/qmark.css; this page links the stylesheet and pastes the markup, so what is below is the component and not a drawing of it.

01 · the ladder

Every size the mark has to survive

brand/BRAND.md §3 gives the icon mark its job: legible at 16–180px. Hit replay and watch the same component at each of them. The electron renders at 16% of the mark — 3.84px at 24, 3.2px at 20, 2.56px at 16 — and watching it at each: at 24 it is a distinct point travelling a ring; at 20 that is marginal; at 16 it is not an orbit at all, it is the mark wobbling. So at and below 20px the orbit does not run and those rungs show the settled Q from the first frame. Nothing else about the mark changes.

04 · asked not to move

The settled mark

What someone who has asked for stillness sees, and what every rung under 20px above shows from the first frame: the mark, arrived. Not a faster orbit — no orbit. It is the same markup with the animation switched off, which is possible only because the reveal's last frame is the identity transform: there is no position to restore, because the electron never left the DOM's.

To see the real path, not this stand-in: turn on Reduce motion (macOS System Settings → Accessibility → Display) and reload. Every mark on this page, including the ladder above, should be settled at (71,71) from the first frame and never move.
  • What is still open, and is not mine: ring thickness, mono luminance and wordmark kerning — brand/BRAND.md §5. They are --qmark-ring-width and --qmark-ink, carrying the lab's current positions. The motion depends on neither: it depends on the ring's radius and the electron's centre, and those were settled on 2026-06-27.
  • What the motion costs: one transform on one <g>, inside an <svg> whose box is fixed. No length, margin, font-size or colour animates, so it cannot move anything on the page it lands in — including a reading row.
05 · where it goes

The app's menu bar, before and after

The app's bar carries the brand device — 8px of the declared green — beside the wordmark. The icon mark is what BRAND.md §2 calls "the main gap", and this is the swap: the dot becomes the Q, at the component's default 1.5rem, which is also the smallest size at which the reveal is worth running. Not applied — app/index.html is being changed by someone else — so what is below is the bar rebuilt from its own tokens, and the wiring is named rather than done.
